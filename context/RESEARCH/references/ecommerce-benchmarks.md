# E-commerce benchmarks (owner screenshots, deconstructed 2026-09-12)

> ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES ONLY. The owner shared two screenshots purely
> as context so agents understand the quality bar and how design
> posture shifts with niche. These are NOT the product, NOT build
> targets — no RFC/SPEC may treat them as requirements. The actual
> niche and content are defined per-project by the RFC.

Future heroes, grids, and trust strips are judged on craftsmanship
at this level, adapted to whatever niche the RFC names.

## A. WEAVVI — POD pet (playful archetype)
Announcement strip → big emotional H1 ("for people whose best friend
has four legs") + subcopy + dual CTA + rating chips → navy trust ribbon
→ "Pick your pack" circular category nav → "Top picks for pet parents"
4-col grid (badge, title, price, rating) → trust-icon row (COD, free
shipping, 7-day returns, secure payments) → segmented "For dog people /
For cat people" grids → stats band (10,000+ / 4.8 / 30+ / 24h) →
editorial banner ("No meal eaten alone") → review cards with names →
FAQ accordion ("Questions? We've got treats": shipping, COD, fit,
print, care) → bold CTA ("Find the tee your pet would pick") + fat
footer + newsletter. Motifs: paw/confetti background, pastel bands,
rounded 16–24px cards, sticker badges, marquee strip.

## B. Furniture — premium (craft archetype)
Dark hero ("Made for homes and workspaces") + bulk-enquiry CTA →
circular category nav → trust strip (est. 2020, quality, easy
enquiries, bulk support) → "Crafted moments" editorial collage +
"Explore more" tile → Best Sellers grid with Add-to-cart → Deals of
the Day on burnt-orange band + countdown → wide lifestyle banners →
New Arrivals → Shop by category → Trending Now → Our Story + masonry
gallery → bulk CTA band → newsletter + fat footer. Motifs: generous
whitespace, 4–8px radii, material photography, single restrained
accent, muted urgency ("In stock / Made to order / Bulk quote").

## What "damn good" means here
Real catalog density (grids, not 3-card marketing), proof near every
claim, every section ending in a CTA or next step, zero dead-ends,
micro-motion on every card/button, background rhythm alternating
band → grid → editorial so the page never reads as identical stacked
boxes. Stats/names/counts above are UNVERIFIED references — never
ship them as store facts; source from `context/DATA/` or merchant.
