# Glossary (shared client ↔ agent vocabulary)

## Variant

A specific version of a product (e.g. size M, blue) with its own price and stock.

## Collection

A group of products shown together (e.g. "New arrivals").

## Metafield

Extra structured info on a product (e.g. fabric, size guide) editable in Shopify admin.

## Section / Block

Theme-editor building pieces: sections are full-width rows, blocks are the
reorderable items inside them. The client composes pages from these — no code needed.

## Cart drawer

The sliding mini-cart that opens when a shopper adds a product.
